Scope: tracks the people in a short video and predicts four person–person relationships — behind, in front of, next to, watching — on every third frame, kept in a temporal graph. It does not recognise other objects or answer questions about the video.

Advanced settings

A prediction counts only if its raw EGTR score is at least this value. Raw scores are uncalibrated ranking scores, usually far below 0.01. The default was chosen on development videos; it is not a validated operating point.

Relationships are predicted on every third frame. A retained relationship stays this many steps after its last prediction.

    Loads both inference environments and checks the GPU (usually under a minute). No models are downloaded.

      Before you rely on a result

      • Relationships come from EGTR, an image model trained on still photos; each frame is predicted independently.
      • Scores are raw ranking scores, not probabilities. Empty or wrong graphs are possible and are shown as they are.
      • GatedEMA and V-HPED are experimental: on raw scores they usually return empty graphs.
      • The research study found no evidence that the temporal methods improve real predicted relationships.
